# The Breakup

Ryan texted me at midnight. He said we needed to talk. I knew right away what that meant.

The next day we met at a coffee shop downtown. He was already there, scrolling on his phone. He looked nervous. He said he wasn't ready for something serious. He said it wasn't me, it was him.

I wanted to cry but I didn't. I just nodded and said okay. Then I walked home in the rain and ate a whole pint of ice cream.

My roommate thought I was overreacting. Maybe I was. But it still hurt.
