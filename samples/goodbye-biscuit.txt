# Goodbye, Biscuit

We had to put our dog Biscuit down on Saturday. She was fourteen years old. She was a beagle mix that we got from the shelter when I was in first grade.

She was the best dog ever. She was so loyal and so sweet and she loved everybody. She will always be in our hearts and she is in a better place now. I know she's not in pain anymore.

She had this thing where she'd wait by the door at exactly 3:20 every day because that's when the bus dropped me off. Even after I started driving to school she still waited at 3:20. My mom says she did it until last week. She also stole socks but never chewed them, she just put them in her bed like she was collecting them.

The house is so quiet now. Her leash is still on the hook. Nobody wants to be the one to move it. Rest in peace Biscuit. We love you and we will never forget you.
