# Later Start Times

School starts at 7:30. That is too early. Teenagers are tired. They do not get enough sleep. Their bodies want to sleep late. Scientists have studied this. They say school should start later. First period is a nightmare. Half the class is asleep. The teacher talks to zombies. Nobody learns anything. Some kids drive to school tired. That is dangerous. Some kids skip breakfast. That is unhealthy.

People say later start times would mess up sports. Practice would end later. Buses would cost more. Parents would have to change their schedules. These are real problems. But they can be solved.

At the end of the day, health is more important than convenience. The early bird gets the worm, but the tired bird gets nothing. School should start at 8:30. It is time for a change.
