# Thank You to Ms. Ortiz

Dear Ms. Ortiz,

I just wanted to say thank you so much for everything this year. You were an amazing teacher and I really appreciate everything you did for me. You always went above and beyond.

When I started your class I really didn't like writing. I thought I was bad at it. But you made it fun and you always believed in me. I learned so much from you and I will always remember your class.

I especially want to thank you for staying after school with me before the essay was due. You didn't have to do that. You read my draft three times and you told me my conclusion was just my introduction again with different words, which was true. I was kind of embarrassed but I fixed it and I got a B+, which was my best grade all year.

Thank you again for everything. You made a real difference in my life. Have a great summer!

Sincerely,
Marcus
