# The Party

The party was at Jessica's house. Her parents were out of town. Everyone from school was there and the music was super loud.

I stood in the kitchen with my friend Emma. We didn't know many people. Then this tall guy came over and started talking to us. His name was Chris. He was kind of arrogant but also funny.

He said the party was lame and the people were boring. Emma rolled her eyes. I thought he was rude. But later I saw him helping clean up when everyone else left. Maybe I was wrong about him.
