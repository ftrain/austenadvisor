# Grandma's Banana Bread

This is my grandma's banana bread recipe and it's honestly the best one ever. First, make sure you preheat the oven to 350. Oh and you need really ripe bananas, like the ones that are almost black, otherwise it won't taste right, so you should buy them about a week before.

Mash three bananas in a bowl. Add a stick of melted butter. Add a cup of sugar. Add an egg. Add a teaspoon of vanilla. Add a teaspoon of baking soda and a pinch of salt. Then stir in a cup and a half of flour. Don't overmix it or it gets tough. Actually you should mix the dry stuff in a separate bowl first, I forgot.

Pour it into a greased loaf pan. Bake it for about an hour, or until a toothpick comes out clean. My grandma never used a toothpick, she just knew when it was done by the smell, but you probably should.

Let it cool before you cut it. Nobody ever does though.
