# Causes of the American Revolution

There were a lot of causes of the American Revolution, the first one was taxes, Britain had a big debt from the French and Indian War so they taxed the colonies with things like the Stamp Act and the tax on tea and the colonists were mad because they didn't have anyone in Parliament, which is where "no taxation without representation" comes from. Then there was the Boston Massacre where soldiers shot some people and Paul Revere made a picture of it that made it look worse than it was. Then the Boston Tea Party happened and they threw the tea in the harbor. Britain punished Boston with the Intolerable Acts and closed the port. Then there was fighting at Lexington and Concord. So basically all of these things together caused the Revolution. Also the Enlightenment.
