# First Day at Westfield

My first day at Westfield High was basically a disaster. I got there late because the bus app said 7:15 and the bus actually came at 7:05. So I walked in during first period and everyone turned around and looked at me.

The school is huge compared to my old one. There are three floors and the room numbers make no sense. I got lost twice. A teacher yelled at me for being in the hallway without a pass even though I literally didn't know where I was going.

At lunch I didn't know where to sit. I ended up sitting at the end of a table with some kids who were playing a card game. They didn't talk to me but they didn't tell me to leave either. One of them gave me a chip.

Honestly the day wasn't that bad. I'm actually kind of excited to go back tomorrow. The teachers seem nice and I think I'm going to like it here.
