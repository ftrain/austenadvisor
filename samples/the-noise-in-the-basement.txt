# The Noise in the Basement

It was around midnight and I was home alone because my parents were at my aunt's thing. I was watching a show on my laptop when I heard a noise in the basement. It was a kind of scraping noise. I was really scared. My heart was beating really fast.

I paused the show and listened. Nothing. Then I heard it again. I thought about calling my mom but I didn't want to seem like a baby. I grabbed the flashlight from the kitchen drawer and I went to the top of the stairs. The basement was totally dark.

I turned on the flashlight and went down slowly. I was so scared I could barely breathe. At the bottom I shined the light around and it was just the cat. She had knocked over a box of old Christmas stuff. I felt really stupid but also really relieved. Then my phone buzzed and it was my mom asking why I'd called her three times.
