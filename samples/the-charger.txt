# The Charger

Me and my sister got in a huge fight last night and it was over a phone charger, which sounds dumb but it wasn't really about the charger.

She took mine because hers was broken, which she does all the time, and she didn't ask, and when I asked for it back she said she was using it and I could wait, and I said it's literally mine, and she said stop being so dramatic, and then my mom came in and told both of us to stop, but she only looked at me when she said it, which is what always happens.

The thing is she's older so she always gets away with everything. She gets the front seat, she gets to stay out later, she got her own room first. Mom says it's because she's older but I think it's because she's the favorite.

Anyway I took the charger back while she was in the shower. She hasn't said anything to me today. I don't really care.
