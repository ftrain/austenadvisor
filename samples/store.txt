# A Trip to the Store

I needed milk. So I grabbed my keys and drove to the store. It was super crowded. A guy in a hoodie was blocking the whole aisle while he talked on his phone.

I didn't want to be rude. I said excuse me. He totally ignored me. Finally he moved and I got the milk. Then I saw my ex at the checkout and I basically panicked.

"Hey," she said. "Long time no see. You look great."

"Thanks," I said. "You too." I paid and left as fast as I could. Honestly, it was the most awkward ten minutes of my week.
