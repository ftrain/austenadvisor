# The Layover

Our flight to Denver was supposed to leave at 6 am so we got up at 3:30 which was already terrible. Then at the gate they said it was delayed an hour because of weather. Then it was delayed two hours. Then they said it was a mechanical issue and not the weather at all.

By the time we took off we had missed our connection in Chicago. The airline app kept saying "rebooking in progress" for like four hours. My dad was on hold with customer service the whole time. My mom kept saying "it is what it is."

We ended up sleeping on the floor at O'Hare next to a charging station. I didn't sleep at all. A guy next to us was eating tuna out of a can at 2 in the morning.

We finally got to Denver the next afternoon. Our luggage got there two days after that. My dad still gets mad when anyone says the word Chicago. Looking back it was actually kind of a fun adventure.
