# Homework Should Be Banned

Homework should be banned. First of all it takes forever. Last night I had math, a lab report, and a reading log, and I didn't finish until almost midnight. I always do all of my homework, even when it's pointless, because that's the kind of student I am.

Second, nobody actually learns anything from it. Most kids just copy the answers from the group chat or use an app that solves it for them. I usually skip the worksheets anyway and just study before the test, and I get good grades, so that proves it doesn't matter.

Third, students need time for other things like sports, jobs, family, and sleep. Teenagers are supposed to get nine hours of sleep and almost nobody does.

Some people say homework teaches responsibility. But you can learn responsibility in other ways too. That is why homework should be banned.
