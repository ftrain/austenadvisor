# Summary of the Lecture on Sleep

The guest speaker on Zoom yesterday talked about sleep and why teenagers need more of it. She started by asking everyone how many hours they slept last night, and most people put like six in the chat, and someone put two, which I think was a joke.

She explained that during sleep your brain sorts out what you learned during the day. Then she got sidetracked talking about her dog, who apparently sleeps sixteen hours a day, and there was a picture of him on a couch. Then her audio cut out for a while so I didn't catch the part about melatonin.

When it came back she was talking about phones and blue light. Then she said something about dreams and how nobody really knows why we have them. I think also she said caffeine stays in your system for a long time.

At the end Mr. Okafor thanked her and reminded us the reading response is due Friday. It was an interesting lecture.
