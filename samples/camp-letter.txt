# Letter Home from Camp

Dear Mom and Dad,

Camp is okay I guess. The food is gross and my bunkmate snores like crazy. Yesterday we went canoeing and I flipped the canoe and everybody laughed. It was so embarrassing.

But there's this girl named Kayla who's really cool. She's from Ohio and she knows a ton about horses. We hang out at the lake after dinner and talk about stuff. I think she likes me but I'm not sure.

Can you send more snacks? And my phone charger, which I totally forgot. The counselors are kind of strict but mostly fine.

Love, Jake

P.S. Please don't let Max use my room.
