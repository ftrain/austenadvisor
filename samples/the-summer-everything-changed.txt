# The Summer Everything Changed

My family moved to Ohio the summer I turned twelve. It was a really hard time for all of us. My dad got a new job and my mom was sad about leaving her friends. My little brother cried a lot. I was angry at everyone.

The new house was bigger but it felt empty. I didn't know anybody on our street. I spent most of the summer in my room on my phone texting my old friends, but they kind of stopped answering after a while. That made me feel even worse.

Then in August I met a girl named Priya who lived two houses down. She was the same age as me and she was also new. We became best friends basically overnight. After that everything got better and I realized that moving wasn't the end of the world. Sometimes the worst things that happen to you turn out to be the best things.
