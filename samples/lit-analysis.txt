# The Green Door

In the story, the main character Nora walks past a green door every day on her way to work. She never opens it. Her mother used to tell her not to go inside when she was little. Then one day near the end of the story the door is open and she looks in and it's just an empty room with a chair. She sits in the chair and cries and then she goes to work.

The green door is a symbol. It represents something. The author uses it a lot, like it comes up maybe five or six times. Green is the color of jealousy and also of nature, so it could mean either one. The empty room is also important.

Overall the author does a really good job using symbolism to show Nora's feelings, and that makes the story more meaningful for the reader.
