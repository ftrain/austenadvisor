# Teenagers and Screen Time

Many teenagers spend a lot of time on screens every day. This can affect their sleep, their mood, and their schoolwork. Some experts think it is a serious problem, while others think the effects are exaggerated. "The relationship between screen use and adolescent well-being is real but small, and it depends heavily on what the screen is being used for" (Hartwell 42). Another thing is that a lot of teens use their phones to stay in touch with friends, which can actually be a good thing. Video games can also help with problem-solving skills. On the other hand, scrolling late at night can keep you up. In my opinion, parents should set limits but not ban phones completely. Overall, screen time has both positive and negative effects on teenagers, and more research needs to be done.
