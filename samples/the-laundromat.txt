# The Laundromat

The laundromat on Fifth Street is a really interesting place. It's open 24 hours and there's always something going on there. It's kind of old and run down but it has a lot of character.

When you walk in you see a lot of washing machines. There are also a lot of dryers. The walls are yellow. There is a TV in the corner. There are plastic chairs. There is a machine that sells soap. The floor is gray. It smells like detergent.

There's always a lot of different people there. Sometimes there's a mom with her kids. Sometimes there's college students. There's an old man who comes every Sunday and folds his shirts very carefully, like each one is important, and he never looks at his phone the whole time.

The lights flicker sometimes. It's very loud because of the machines.

I go there every week because our building's machine is broken. I don't love it but I don't hate it either. It's just one of those places that is part of my life.
