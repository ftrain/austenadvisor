# Why We Should Read Frankenstein

Our class should definitely read Frankenstein next year. It's about a scientist named Victor who wants to make a person out of dead body parts. He goes to college and gets obsessed and stops writing to his family. Then he finally makes the creature and it opens its eyes and he totally freaks out and runs away.

The creature wanders around and learns to talk by spying on a family in a cottage. He reads some books and figures out he's alone. Then he asks Victor to make him a wife, and Victor starts to but then rips it up. After that a lot of people die and they chase each other to the North Pole.

It's a really good book and it's way more interesting than the movies make it look. Plus it's short-ish. So that's why we should read it.
