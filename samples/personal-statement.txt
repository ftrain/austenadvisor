# Personal Statement

Ever since I was a little kid, I have always been passionate about helping people. I think this is because of my grandmother, who was a nurse for thirty years and always told me that kindness is the most important thing in the world.

In high school I joined a lot of clubs. I was in Key Club, the Spanish Honor Society, and Model UN, and I volunteered at the food pantry on Saturdays. I also worked part time at a pizza place. These experiences taught me leadership, teamwork, and time management. They also taught me that hard work pays off.

One time at the food pantry a woman came in with her three kids and she was really embarrassed. I helped her pick out food and talked to her kids and by the end she was smiling. It was a life-changing moment for me.

I want to study nursing at your university because I want to make a difference in the world, just like my grandmother did. I know I will be a great fit for your community.
