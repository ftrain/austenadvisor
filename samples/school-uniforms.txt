# Uniforms Are a Bad Idea

School uniforms are a terrible idea and our school should never make us wear them. Uniforms take away students' freedom of expression. They make everyone look exactly the same, like robots. They are uncomfortable and nobody likes them. They cost a lot of money and families shouldn't have to pay for them. They don't stop bullying, because bullies will always find something to make fun of. They don't make students learn better. They are old-fashioned and most schools are getting rid of them anyway. Students already have enough rules to follow. Everybody knows that kids do better when they can be themselves. If the school forces uniforms on us, students will be miserable and grades will go down. It's common sense. Uniforms are just a bad idea all around.
