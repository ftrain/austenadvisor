# Social Media and Friendship

I got my first phone in sixth grade and joined the group chat for my grade the same night. At first it was fun. People sent memes and talked about shows and planned stuff on weekends. I felt like I knew everybody.

In seventh grade my best friend Leah moved across the country. We texted every day and watched each other's stories, and for a while it felt like nothing had changed. Then she started posting pictures with new friends I didn't know, and I started posting more too, kind of to show her I was fine.

By eighth grade we only reacted to each other's posts. We never actually talked. When she came back to visit last summer, we sat at a coffee shop and both kept checking our phones because we didn't know what to say.

So I think social media doesn't really keep friendships alive, it just makes it look like they're alive when they're actually over.
