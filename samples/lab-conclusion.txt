# Lab Conclusion: Plants and Light

The purpose of this lab was to see if light affects how plants grow. We had two bean plants. Plant A was put by the window and Plant B was put in the closet. We watered them both the same amount every day, or most days, except over the long weekend.

After two weeks Plant A was 14 cm and Plant B was 9 cm. Plant B was also kind of yellow and droopy. This proves that plants need light to grow. Our hypothesis was correct.

Some errors were that we forgot to measure on Friday and also someone in the other group knocked over Plant A so it might have been damaged. We will also note that the closet plant grew taller at first, which is weird. If we did this lab again we would use more plants and be more careful. Overall the lab was a success and we learned a lot about photosynthesis.
