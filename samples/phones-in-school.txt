# Phones in School

Phones are a really big part of our lives today. Basically everyone has one, even kids in middle school. Some schools have started banning them and putting them in lockers or those little pouches that lock. A lot of people have strong opinions about this.

Teachers say phones are distracting. Parents say they need to reach their kids in an emergency. Students say it's not fair because adults use their phones all day at work. All of these people kind of have a point.

Also phones can be used for learning. You can look stuff up, use a calculator, take a picture of the board, and check the assignment on the class app. My friend uses hers to record the Spanish teacher so she can practice at home.

In conclusion, I think phones should be allowed in school but with rules, because banning them doesn't really solve anything and students need to learn how to use them responsibly.
