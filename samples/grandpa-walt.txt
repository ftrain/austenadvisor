# Grandpa Walt

My grandpa Walt is the most interesting person I know. He was in the Navy and he fixed engines on ships. After that he owned a hardware store for thirty years. He is 84 now and he still drives, which my mom is not happy about.

He is a very wise person and he has taught me a lot of things. He always says that you should do things right the first time. He is also very funny. He does crossword puzzles in pen.

Every summer I stay with him for two weeks. We get up early and he makes eggs that are always kind of burnt. Then we work in the garage on whatever he's fixing. Last summer it was a lawnmower. He doesn't talk much while he works but when I mess up he just hands me the tool again without saying anything.

I think he is the reason I want to be an engineer. He has been a huge role model in my life and I love him very much.
