# New Neighbors

A new family moved in next door last weekend. They have a huge truck and two little kids. The dad seems nice. The mom is kind of standoffish.

My mother baked them cookies right away. She wanted to find out everything about them. Where they came from, what they did for work, whether they went to church.

It turns out the dad is a lawyer and he's single. Well, divorced. My mom got this look on her face. I knew exactly what she was thinking. She has been trying to set me up for years.
