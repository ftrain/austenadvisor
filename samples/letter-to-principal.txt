# A Letter to the Principal

Dear Dr. Alvarez,

I'm writing because I am really frustrated about what happened in the cafeteria on Tuesday. A bunch of us were sitting at our usual table and a lunch aide told us we had to move because the table was "reserved." Nobody had ever said anything about reserved tables before. When we asked why, she said that was just the rule now and to stop arguing.

This isn't the first time something like this has happened. Last month the library suddenly closed during lunch with no warning, and the vending machines have been broken since basically September.

Students feel like rules just appear out of nowhere and nobody explains them. It makes people not trust the administration. When the aide yelled at us on Thursday, two of my friends almost got detention for asking a question.

Could you please post the cafeteria rules somewhere so we know what they are?

Thank you,
Jordan Lee, Grade 10
