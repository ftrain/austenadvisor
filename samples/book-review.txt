# Book Review: The Lighthouse Year

The Lighthouse Year is honestly one of the best books I have ever read. I couldn't put it down. I read the whole thing in two days, mostly on the bus and at night when I was supposed to be sleeping.

The characters are amazing and so realistic. The main character is relatable and you really feel what she feels. Her little brother is hilarious. The writing is beautiful and the descriptions make you feel like you're actually there. The plot has so many twists and the ending made me cry.

Some people might say it's slow in the middle, but I didn't think so. It's a coming-of-age story but it's also about family and friendship and growing up.

I would give it five out of five stars. If you like good books, you will love this one. Everyone should read it.
