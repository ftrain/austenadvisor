# Review: Wireless Earbuds

I would give these earbuds 3 stars. They're okay but not great.

The sound quality is actually really good. The bass is strong and you can hear everything in the song clearly. I use them mostly for podcasts on the train and for my workouts. They pair with my phone pretty fast.

But the battery is terrible. The box says eight hours but I get maybe four. Also the case is kind of cheap feeling and the lid already got loose after a month. And the left one keeps falling out when I run, even with the bigger tips. I've almost lost it twice on the treadmill.

The noise cancelling is fine I guess. It's not as good as the expensive ones but it's way cheaper so that makes sense.

Overall I would recommend them. They're a good value. If you want good earbuds for not a lot of money these are definitely worth it. Just don't expect them to stay in your ears or last all day.
