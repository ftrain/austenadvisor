# A Complaint to a Blender Company

To Whom It May Concern,

I am writing to complain about the VortexPro 5000 blender that I bought from your website on March 3rd. I am very disappointed and frankly I expected a lot better from a company like yours.

I have been a loyal customer for many years. I also own your toaster and your coffee maker and I have always recommended your products to my friends and family. That is why this is so upsetting to me.

The blender worked fine for about two weeks. Then it started making a grinding noise. Then last Sunday it started smoking while I was making a smoothie and the whole kitchen smelled like burning plastic. My daughter was right there. Somebody could have gotten hurt.

I called your customer service line twice and was on hold for 45 minutes both times before the call dropped. I would like a full refund. I also think you should look into whether this is a safety issue. I look forward to hearing from you as soon as possible.

Sincerely,
Dana Whitfield
