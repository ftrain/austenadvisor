# Rebuttal: The Four-Day School Week

My opponent says a four-day week would save money and give students more time to rest. But honestly, has my opponent ever actually looked at the numbers? I don't think so. Their whole speech was basically just feelings.

They also said teachers would be happier. Okay, but who cares if teachers are happier if students are falling behind? That's the real issue here. And they talked about a district in another state where it worked, but that's just one district, and things are totally different there.

Also, what are working parents supposed to do on Fridays? My opponent didn't even mention that. That shows they didn't think it through.

Plus, kids would just stay on their phones all day on the extra day off. Everybody knows that's what would happen. So for all these reasons, my opponent's case falls apart, and you should vote for our side.
