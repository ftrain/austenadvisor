# The Interview

The office was huge and super modern. Everyone wore hoodies and drank fancy coffee. I felt totally out of place in my suit.

The manager was a woman named Priya. She was friendly but kind of intense. She asked me about my strengths and weaknesses. I said I was a perfectionist, which is a lame answer. She smiled anyway.

After the interview I called my sister. She said I probably nailed it. I hope she's right because I really need this job. Rent is due next week and I'm basically broke.
