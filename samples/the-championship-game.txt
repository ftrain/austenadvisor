# The Championship Game

So we made it to the championship which was huge because our team hasn't won anything in like ten years. Everyone was nervous. Coach gave a speech before the game about giving 110 percent and leaving it all on the field.

The first half was really bad. We were down 14 to 3. The other team was bigger than us and faster. Our quarterback threw an interception. Then he threw another one. At halftime nobody said anything in the locker room.

The second half was a lot better. We scored a touchdown. Then we scored a field goal. Then the defense got a sack. Then we scored another touchdown. The crowd was going crazy. With two minutes left we were winning 17 to 14. They had the ball but our defense held them.

We won. It was the best day of my life. I will never forget it. It just shows that if you never give up anything is possible.
