# A Toast for Sam and Jules

Hi everyone, for those who don't know me I'm Ben, I've been Sam's best friend since third grade. I'm not really good at speeches so bear with me.

I looked up the word love in the dictionary to get ready for this. Just kidding. But seriously, Sam and Jules are perfect for each other. They complete each other. They're each other's other half.

When Sam first met Jules he called me at 1 am and talked for an hour. I've known Sam for twenty years and he has never called me after ten o'clock for anything. He doesn't even text after ten. That's how I knew.

Jules, you make him a better person. Sam, you make her laugh harder than anyone I've ever seen. Marriage is hard but I know you guys can get through anything together.

Anyway I'm rambling. So everyone raise your glasses. To Sam and Jules. May your love last forever and ever. Cheers!
